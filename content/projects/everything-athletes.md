Everything Athletes was an athlete mental-health platform I founded and built from concept to market, owning product strategy, research, operations, partnerships and launch.

I turned market and user research into products and resources, including a research-driven book that sold more than 1,000 copies, and built partnerships with sports psychologists, media, PR firms and universities. Earned media and speaking engagements extended its reach.

It offered online courses, a podcast, book reviews and a blog about athlete mental health.

I also built content for the brand’s social media engine across platforms, from short-form video to community conversation, so the work didn’t just sit on a website. People could find it where they already were.

Over time I developed Everything Athletes into a multi-channel brand, generating content across audio, video and multimedia for both the brand and the platform: Instagram (3,000+ followers), TikTok, a podcast and a Shopify store.

Day to day, that meant running the roadmap, user research, budget, contractors, QA and content: the full stack of getting something from idea to audience.
