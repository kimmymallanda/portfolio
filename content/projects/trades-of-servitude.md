**Logline:** On his eighteenth birthday, an adopted teenager learns that his birth parents were murdered by a crime boss who now lives in Brussels under a new name, so he enrolls in butler school and works his way into the man’s household, only to find himself falling for the family he came to destroy.

## Synopsis

In the middle of his adoptive parents’ divorce, Jacob is handed a file about his birth parents and the man who killed them: a crime boss who escaped prison and disappeared overseas. Jacob wants one thing, to see his face. With his best friend Maxwell, he spends his graduation money on two plane tickets to Brussels.

Under the name “Jacobo,” he enrolls at a butler academy in the Netherlands and ends up inside the household of Mr. Chow, the man he came for. Revenge plays out in his imagination in vivid, grisly detail. In reality, he is drawn to the family living under Chow’s roof, and has to decide what he actually came there to do.
