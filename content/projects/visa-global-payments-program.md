Visa’s global payments program is complicated: many products, many regions, and many teams that all have to agree before anything ships.

My work is to make it understandable and repeatable. I redesigned the requirements, workflows, ownership and documentation behind client onboarding, translated enterprise client needs into product and operational solutions, and found the root causes behind escalations so they could be fixed at the source.

I also identified places where AI and automation could replace manual work, and rapidly prototyped practical solutions, including a knowledge agent that centralized the program’s complex information.
