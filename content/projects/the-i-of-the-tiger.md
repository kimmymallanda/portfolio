**The I of the Tiger: The Athlete Identity & Remedying Sport’s Greatest Conflicts** asks what it means to be an athlete, and why mental health in sports matters so much.

Written by a retired athlete and sports researcher, the book analyzes the psychology behind forming an athlete identity and its impact on mental health in today’s sports world. It guides readers through the toughest moments athletes face along their journey (defeat, injury and retirement) and how to get through them.

It brings together personal knowledge and top-rated research, and offers practical ideas, narratives and approaches for working through mental blocks, struggles and irrational beliefs. The argument is simple: ignoring athletes’ emotional struggle in modern competition is an outdated, harmful approach to sport, and it’s time to challenge the stigma around mental health.

## About the author

Kimberly Carducci earned her degree from the University of North Carolina and competed in Division I swimming at the University of North Carolina. Since retiring from full-time sports, she has been an advocate for mental health in sports, and founded Everything Athletes, an athlete mental-health platform she ran from 2022 to 2024.
