Bellwood Labs is a software development company that works with clients across FinTech, law, retail, entertainment, non-profits, medical and higher education. This one-pager introduces the company in a single page.

It leads with the line “Dreamer. Thinkers. Builders.” over an Atlanta skyline, then organizes the essentials into scannable blocks: mission, core values, industries served and clients.

I designed the entire page in two versions; the second adds the circular badge logo at the top.
