Leaving competitive sport is one of the hardest transitions an athlete makes, and most people hit it without a guide. This eBook was a short, abbreviated version of the full Everything Athletes course on navigating it.

It moves through four ideas: finding people who understand what you’re going through, getting closure, letting go of the athlete label, and discovering who you are outside sport. Each section pairs a few plain-spoken pages with a small exercise, such as writing a letter to your sport, and points to places to find support, including the brand’s anonymous Reddit community.

Every page links back to the full online course, so the eBook did two jobs at once: it helped the reader, and it introduced them to the brand.
